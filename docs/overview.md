# Business Goal 
Build a lakehouse pipeline for an e-commerce business that supports: 
- Customer Analytics 
- Product Analytics 
- Sales Analytics 
- Payment Monitoring 
- Inventory Monitoring 

Business Questions: 
1. What is total revenue by month?
2. Which products generate the most revenue?
3. What is the current inventory level?
4. Which products are low in stock?
5. How long do orders take to move from placed to delivered?
6. What is the payment success rate?
7. How have product prices changed over time? 

# Source systems 
Total source systems(From where data comes): 1 E-Commerce Application 

Tables: 
1. customers_raw 
2. products_raw 
3. orders_raw 
4. order_items_raw 
5. payments_raw 
6. payments_raw
